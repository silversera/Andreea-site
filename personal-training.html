FAQ

Helpful answers before you start.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. Sessions can start from the basics, with clear coaching, simple progressions, and a pace that feels comfortable for you.

Do I need to be fit before starting?

No. You do not need to get fit first. Personal training is designed to meet you where you are and build from there.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions are available in Portlaoise, with client home-based training where suitable. Online and hybrid coaching options may also be available depending on your goals and setup.

Can personal training include Pilates-style movement?

Yes. Sessions can include Pilates-style movement, breath, core work, mobility, posture, strength, and controlled transitions.

How do I get started?

Book a consultation through the contact section or message Andreea on WhatsApp. You can discuss your goals, current level, and the best coaching option for you.

Ready to feel stronger and more confident?

Start with a friendly consultation and choose the training option that feels right for you.
