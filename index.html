Personal Training & Pilates Coaching in Portlaoise

“Train with control, move with rhythm.”

As both a Personal Trainer and a certified Pilates Instructor, I combine traditional strength training with Pilates to create balanced, effective sessions adapted to your goals, your body’s needs, and your energy levels. I bring personalised training to the comfort and privacy of your own home, with all equipment provided, helping you build strength, move with greater ease, and feel more connected to your body. You’ll also receive short personalised practice routines between sessions to support your progress and help you stay consistent.

Meet Your Movement Coach

Personal training with rhythm, control and care.

Gina Andreea Serafim

REPs Ireland Registered Personal Trainer, Fitness Coach and Mat Pilates Instructor.

Movement has shaped my life—from professional dance to personal training and Pilates. Today, I combine those experiences to help people build strength, move with greater control, and enjoy exercising in a way that feels supportive and sustainable. Whether you're taking your first steps into fitness or looking to complement the training you already do, every session is designed around your goals and how you want to move. My role is to guide you with clear instruction, thoughtful progression, and genuine encouragement. My goal isn't simply to help you exercise. It's to help you understand your body, trust your movement, and build strength and confidence that support your everyday life.
